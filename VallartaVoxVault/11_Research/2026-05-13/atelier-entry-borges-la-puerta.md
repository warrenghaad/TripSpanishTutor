> "La puerta" — the door, the gate, the threshold.

## Meaning
A single noun carrying weight: not "a" door but **the** door — a particular threshold the speaker already knows.

## Literal
`la-puerta` — the (feminine) door.

## Natural
"La puerta" — said with the assumption you already know which one.

## Grammar Skeleton
The Spanish definite article `la` does heavier work than English "the." It marks the noun as already-known to both speakers — a shared referent. Borges leans on this to make ordinary nouns feel mythic: by saying *la* puerta, he points at a door you and he both already remember.

## Practice Move
Say `la puerta` aloud, then in your head place a real door behind it (your apartment, the airport gate, the bookshop in Vallarta). Notice how the article changes the door from generic to specific.

- **back:** the door / the gate / the threshold
