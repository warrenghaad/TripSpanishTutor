# 07 Creative Writing

Drafts, fragments, ode attempts, micro-scenes. The output of practice from `06_Atelier`.

Nothing here has to be good. It has to be *yours*, in Spanish.

## Naming

`YYYY-MM-DD slug.md` — e.g. `2026-05-13 oda al taxi.md`

## Suggested templates

- Ode (after Neruda) — `oda a + [noun]`
- Instructions (after Cortázar) — `instrucciones para + [infinitive]`
- One-paragraph scene — write a moment from your day in 6 sentences

## Promotion

When a phrase from a draft earns a permanent home → move it to [[Voice Recovery Phrases]] or a new WordLens note.
