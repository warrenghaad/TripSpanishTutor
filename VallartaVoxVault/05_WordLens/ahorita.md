# ahorita

## Meaning

A diminutive of *ahora* (now). In Mexico, it can mean:

1. **Right now** — *Ahorita lo hago.* (I'm doing it right now.)
2. **In a minute** — *Ahorita voy.* (I'm coming in a sec.)
3. **Eventually / never** — *Ahorita.* (...spoken with a shrug. Don't hold your breath.)

## How to read which

Context, tone, and body language do the work. The same word covers urgency and procrastination.

## Heard

- 2026-05-13 — at the airport — *Ahorita le digo* — meaning probably (2), but possibly (3).

## Use it yourself

- *Ahorita regreso.* — I'll be right back.
- *Ahorita lo busco.* — I'll look for it in a moment.
