# Neruda

> Odas elementales — odes to ordinary things. The *form*, not the famous love poems, is the most useful lab for a voice-first learner.

## How to use Neruda in this workbook

- Steal the **ode form**: address a small thing directly, list its qualities, name what it does to you.
- His vocabulary is concrete: bread, salt, lemon, wood. Perfect for beginner-adult voice.

## Practice move — your own ode

Pick a small thing in your day (a taxi, a key, the airport floor). Write 5 lines:

1. Address it: *Tú, ...*
2. Name what it is: *tú eres + [noun].*
3. Name what it does: *tú haces + [verb].*
4. Name what it gives you: *me das + [noun].*
5. Close with a question: *¿qué serías sin mí?*
