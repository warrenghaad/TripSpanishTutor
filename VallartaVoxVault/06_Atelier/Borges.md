# Borges

> The library is a sphere whose center is anywhere and whose circumference is nowhere.
> — paraphrase of a recurring Borges figure.

## How to use Borges in this workbook

- Borges is a **lab**, not the curriculum.
- His Spanish is high-register, Rioplatense, careful, full of literary doubling.
- His sentences are often very *long*. Don't imitate the length; imitate the *attention*.

## What to mine

- relative clauses (*que*, *cuyo*, *el cual*)
- abstract nouns made concrete (*la eternidad*, *el laberinto*, *el espejo*)
- conditional patterns (*si fuera ... sería ...*)

## Constraint

Quote a line or two with citation. Do not paste full stories or poems into this vault.

## A practice move

Pick one short Borges sentence from a physical book you own. Strip it to its skeleton (subject + verb + main object). Rebuild it twice — once shorter, once longer.
