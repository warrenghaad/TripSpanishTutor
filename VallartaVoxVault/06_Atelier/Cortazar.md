# Cortázar

> *Instrucciones para subir una escalera* — Cortázar takes the most ordinary act and writes a step-by-step manual for it.

## How to use Cortázar in this workbook

- Steal the **instructions** form: imperative + small ordinary action.
- Argentine, so *vos* shows up. Useful for ear training, not for PVR speaking.
- Cortázar's prose is shorter and more playful than Borges — a gentler entry point.

## Practice move — your own instructions

Pick a small action (washing your hands, ordering a coffee, finding your gate). Write 5 instructions in usted command form (more useful for PVR):

1. Primero, ...
2. Después, ...
3. Si hay ..., entonces ...
4. Cuidado con ...
5. Al final, ...

Drop the result in [[07 Creative Writing]].
