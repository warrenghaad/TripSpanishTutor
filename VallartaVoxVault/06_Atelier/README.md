# 06 Atelier

Art, poems, music, film, history clips. The *lab*, not the *curriculum*.

Each author or medium gets a folder. Notes inside follow the same shape: a clipped fragment (with citation), what it taught, a phrase or pattern it produced.

## Constraints

- No full copyrighted poems. Quote one or two lines with citation; paraphrase the rest.
- Every literary item should produce **at least one** of: phrase, practice move, grammar skeleton, saved question, personal echo, JSON card.
- Rioplatense Spanish lives here (under Borges and Cortázar), not in travel notes.
