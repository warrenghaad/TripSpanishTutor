# Magical Realism

The shared room behind García Márquez, Borges, Cortázar, Rulfo, and (in different keys) Allende and Esquivel.

## What to mine for Spanish

- the **imperfect** tense doing the heavy lifting (*era*, *estaba*, *vivía*) — Spanish's natural mode for "things that just kept being true"
- matter-of-fact sentences about impossible facts — practice unflinching declarative Spanish
- proper nouns made into adjectives — *un olor macondiano*

## Practice move

Write three short sentences. In each, state something impossible as if it were ordinary. Use the **imperfect**.

- *Las cartas llegaban antes de ser escritas.*
- *El hotel siempre tenía un cuarto más, aunque estaba lleno.*
- *El gato hablaba español los domingos.*

Voice note: magical realism is grammar plus calm.
