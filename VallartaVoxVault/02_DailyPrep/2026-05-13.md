# DailyPrep: 2026-05-13 — airport_arrival

## Scene

Landing in Puerto Vallarta. Walking off the plane into a Jalisco evening. Customs, baggage, taxi, hotel check-in. The first hour decides whether you arrive in Spanish or arrive in English-pretending-to-be-Spanish.

## Atelier for Today

### Literary resonance

- Borges, *El otro, el mismo* — arriving somewhere is meeting yourself slightly different. Not a lesson, a frame for the day. (Read tonight, not on the plane.)

### Visual / mural context

- PVR's *malecón* and *centro* are mural-dense. Tonight, look up. Save photos to `06_Atelier/Murals_PV/` as you go.

### Music or sound

- The Jalisco accent is brighter and more clipped than the central Mexican news-Spanish. The *s* stays crisp. Mariachi originated here. Expect it audible somewhere within 12 hours.

### Historical layer

- Sleepy fishing port until John Huston shot *La noche de la iguana* (1964). Liz Taylor + Richard Burton + the world's press = the town that exists now. Tourism Spanish here is layered on a still-Jaliscan core.

## Grammar Priority

- **¿Puede + [infinitive]?** — *¿Puede repetir? ¿Puede escribirlo? ¿Puede hablar más despacio?* Workhorse all day.
- **Voy a + [place].** — destination form. *Voy a este hotel. Voy al centro.* Pair with showing the phone screen.

## Conversation Targets

- Greet a customs officer in Spanish and answer *¿adónde va?* without switching to English.
- Ask the taxi price BEFORE getting in: *¿Cuánto cuesta al hotel?*
- Open the hotel front-desk interaction with *Estoy aprendiendo español*. Set the frame; it doesn't beg, it informs.
- Notice one word you don't know. Drop it in [[10 Day 1 Question Catcher]].

## Cultural Briefing

- *Mande* is not subservient — it's the polite way Mexicans ask *what?* when they didn't catch something. Mirroring it back is fine.
- Tipping: 10–15% at restaurants (check if *propina incluida*), 20–50 MXN to bellhops per bag, round up the taxi.
- Don't try to bargain a taxi *aggressively* from the airport — there's a regulated zone fare. Ask the price, accept or refuse, don't haggle hard.
- *Ahorita* will happen to you. When someone says *ahorita*, ask yourself: urgency, soft soon, or polite never? Body language and tone decide.

## Spelunk Seeds

- **mande** — Why this word? Trace its history (theory: from *mandar*, "to command/send" — a colonial humility marker that became a politeness marker).
- **ahorita** — diminutive of *ahora*. The whole emotional spectrum of "now" compressed into one word. Already seeded → [[ahorita]].
- **chido** — Jalisco/CDMX origin? Register marker. Don't use with elders.

## Creative Prompt for Tonight

Three short sentences about the first thing in PVR that surprised you. Use the **imperfect** at least once (*era*, *estaba*, *había*). Drop in [[07 Creative Writing]].
