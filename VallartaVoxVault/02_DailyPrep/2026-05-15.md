## Today's shape

Light Friday. Yesterday three Borges entries from *Fervor de Buenos Aires* landed in the Atelier — *Un patio*, *Calle desconocida*, *La Recoleta*. Today is the day to **carry one of them into your own writing** rather than read another. PV is your ground; Borges is your tuning fork.

The trail `04_Trails/arrabal-etymology.md` walks one word from al-Andalus to Buenos Aires to Vallarta. The bridge it ends on is the move for today: write a Borges-shaped line about a PV *colonia*.

## Phrases to warm up

- *Por la colonia del cerro…* — "Up through the hill colony…"
- *Se cansaron los dos o tres colores de…* — "The two or three colors of … tired themselves out"
- *Penumbra de…* — "Half-light of…"
- *Convencidos de…* — "Convinced of…"

## Atelier focus

*Un patio*. The reflexive of state-change on an inanimate subject (`se cansaron los colores`) is the single most transportable Borges move. Anything in Vallarta with two or three colors at dusk qualifies.
