# 04 Taxi Hotel

### I'm going to Puerto Vallarta

Spanish: Voy a Puerto Vallarta.

English: I'm going to Puerto Vallarta.

Literal: I-go to Puerto Vallarta.

Grammar skeleton: *Voy a + [place].*

When to use: Customs officer, taxi driver, anyone asking your destination.

Practice:
- Voy al hotel.
- Voy al centro.
- Voy a la playa.

Voice note: *a + el = al* always. *Voy a el hotel* → *voy al hotel*.

---

### I'm going to this hotel

Spanish: Voy a este hotel.

English: I'm going to this hotel.

Literal: I-go to this hotel.

Grammar skeleton: *Voy a + [este / esta / estos / estas] + [noun].*

When to use: Show the taxi driver an address, the hotel name, or a screenshot.

Practice:
- Voy a esta dirección.
- Voy a este restaurante.

Voice note: Pair the phrase with showing the screen. The phrase doesn't have to do the whole job.

---

### How much does it cost?

Spanish: ¿Cuánto cuesta?

English: How much does it cost?

Literal: How-much it-costs?

Grammar skeleton: *¿Cuánto cuesta + [optional noun]?*

When to use: Taxis, markets, restaurants. Ask before you ride, before you buy.

Practice:
- ¿Cuánto cuesta al hotel?
- ¿Cuánto cuesta el viaje?
- ¿Cuánto cuesta en total?

Voice note: For multiple items: *¿cuánto cuestan?* (plural).

---

### Can I pay with card?

Spanish: ¿Puedo pagar con tarjeta?

English: Can I pay with a card?

Literal: I-can to-pay with card?

Grammar skeleton: *¿Puedo + [infinitive]?*

When to use: Before sitting down at a restaurant, before getting in a taxi. Cash is still king in PVR for small things.

Practice:
- ¿Aceptan tarjeta?
- ¿Puedo pagar en efectivo?

Voice note: *efectivo* = cash. Many taxis only take cash — always ask first.

---

### The taxi micro-script

```txt
You:    Buenas tardes. Voy a este hotel. [show screen]
Driver: [says price or starts driving]
You:    ¿Cuánto cuesta?
Driver: [price]
You:    Está bien. / ¿Más barato?
```

- *Está bien* = OK / sounds good.
- *¿Más barato?* = cheaper? (used sparingly and friendly, not aggressive).
- *Gracias* at the end always.
