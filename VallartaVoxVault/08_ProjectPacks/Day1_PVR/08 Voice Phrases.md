# 08 Voice Phrases

These are the phrases that keep you *you* in Spanish. Drop them in deliberately — they earn you space.

### I'm learning Spanish

Spanish: Estoy aprendiendo español.

English: I'm learning Spanish.

Literal: I-am learning Spanish.

Grammar skeleton: *Estoy + [-ando/-iendo].*

When to use: Open most interactions on Day 1 with this. People recalibrate.

Practice:
- Estoy aprendiendo, perdón.
- Estoy aprendiendo, pero mi voz funciona.

Voice note: Better than apologizing. Sets the frame instead of begging for it.

---

### My Spanish is still small, but my voice doesn't have to be

Spanish: Mi español todavía es pequeño, pero mi voz no tiene que serlo.

English: My Spanish is still small, but my voice doesn't have to be.

Literal: My Spanish still is small, but my voice not has to be-it.

Grammar skeleton: *Mi [X] es [adj], pero mi [Y] no tiene que serlo.* (*serlo* = "be it/that")

When to use: As a mantra to yourself when you start to shrink. Say it out loud if needed.

Practice:
- Mi vocabulario es pequeño, pero mi idea no es pequeña.

Voice note: The project's north-star phrase. Treat it like the chorus.

---

### I don't want perfect Spanish; I want real Spanish

Spanish: No quiero español perfecto; quiero español real.

English: I don't want perfect Spanish; I want real Spanish.

Literal: Not I-want Spanish perfect; I-want Spanish real.

Grammar skeleton: *No quiero + [noun + adj]; quiero + [noun + adj].*

When to use: Pre-trip self-talk. Also great answer if someone asks why you're learning.

Voice note: Permission slip to be imperfect on purpose.

---

### I like metaphors

Spanish: Me gustan las metáforas.

English: I like metaphors.

Literal: To-me they-please the metaphors.

Grammar skeleton: *Me gusta + [singular].* / *Me gustan + [plural].* The thing liked is the subject.

Practice:
- Me gusta la música.
- Me gustan las palabras raras.

Voice note: *me gustan* (plural) because *metáforas* is plural. The plural ending lives on the verb, not on the *me*.

---

### I'm interested in Latin American literature

Spanish: Me interesa la literatura latinoamericana.

English: I'm interested in Latin American literature.

Literal: To-me interests the literature Latin-American.

Grammar skeleton: *Me interesa + [singular].* / *Me interesan + [plural].*

Practice:
- Me interesan Borges, Neruda y Cortázar.
- Me interesa cómo suena el español.

Voice note: Open door to actual conversation — most Mexicans will run with this if you offer it.

---

### I play guitar / I like the space between the notes

Spanish: Toco guitarra. Me gusta el espacio entre las notas.

English: I play guitar. I like the space between the notes.

Literal: I-play guitar. To-me pleases the space between the notes.

Grammar skeleton: *Toco + [instrument].* / *Me gusta + [noun phrase].*

Practice:
- Toco un poco de jazz.
- Me gusta el silencio en la música.

Voice note: A line that opens conversations with musicians, bartenders, and anyone with an ear.
