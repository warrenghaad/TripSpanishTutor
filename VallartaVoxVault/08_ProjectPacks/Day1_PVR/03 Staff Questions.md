# 03 Staff Questions

Questions to ask hotel, taxi, restaurant, or airport staff.

### What time is check-in?

Spanish: ¿A qué hora es el check-in?

English: What time is check-in?

Literal: At what hour is the check-in?

Grammar skeleton: *¿A qué hora es + [event]?*

When to use: Hotel arrival, restaurant reservation, tour pickup.

Practice:
- ¿A qué hora es el desayuno?
- ¿A qué hora cierra?

Voice note: *check-in* and *check-out* are widely understood in PVR hotels.

---

### Do you have WiFi?

Spanish: ¿Tienen wifi?

English: Do you have WiFi?

Literal: You-all-have wifi?

Grammar skeleton: *¿Tienen + [noun]?* — *tienen* is plural/business "you".

When to use: Café, hotel, restaurant. *Tienen* (plural) is the natural business form.

Practice:
- ¿Tienen el menú en inglés?
- ¿Tienen agua sin gas?

Voice note: *wifi* is pronounced *WEE-fee* in Mexico.

---

### Is there ... near here?

Spanish: ¿Hay ... cerca de aquí?

English: Is there ... near here?

Literal: There-is ... near of here?

Grammar skeleton: *¿Hay + [noun] + cerca?*

When to use: Finding cafés, ATMs, bathrooms, pharmacies.

Practice:
- ¿Hay un cajero cerca?
- ¿Hay una farmacia cerca?
- ¿Hay un baño aquí?

Voice note: *hay* covers both *there is* and *there are*. Never changes.

---

### Could you recommend ...?

Spanish: ¿Me puede recomendar ...?

English: Could you recommend ...?

Literal: To-me you-can to-recommend ...?

Grammar skeleton: *¿Me puede + [infinitive] + [noun]?*

When to use: Restaurants, dishes, taxi drivers, places.

Practice:
- ¿Me puede recomendar un restaurante?
- ¿Me puede recomendar un plato típico?

Voice note: Hotel staff love this question. You'll get a better answer than from any app.

---

### Is the tip included?

Spanish: ¿La propina está incluida?

English: Is the tip included?

Literal: The tip is included?

Grammar skeleton: *¿[Noun] está incluido/incluida?*

When to use: Restaurants. Tips in Mexico are usually 10–15% and often not included.

Practice:
- ¿El impuesto está incluido?
- ¿El desayuno está incluido?

Voice note: Adjective agrees with the noun's gender — *propina* is feminine, so *incluida*.
