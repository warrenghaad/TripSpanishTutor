# 01 Constitution

The non-negotiable layer of the vault. Voice principles, system rules, schemas-by-example. If you ever wonder *"what is this vault for?"*, open this folder.

## What lives here

- **[[Voice Recovery Phrases]]** — phrases that bring you back to yourself in Spanish.
- **Voice/** subfolder — humor, music, photography, sentence-grower scaffolds.
- **Project Principle:** Literature is one lab. Voice is the curriculum.

## What does *not* live here

- Daily content. Those go in `02_DailyPrep` / `03_DailyDebriefs`.
- Vocabulary. That goes in `05_WordLens`.
- Outing prep. That goes in `08_ProjectPacks`.
