# Film Photography Seeing

Vocabulary for talking about how you see — useful for film, photography, murals, light.

## Opening lines

> Me interesa cómo se ve la luz aquí.

> Estoy viendo, no solo mirando.

> La sombra a veces dice más que la forma.

## Verbs of seeing

| Spanish | English | Use |
|---|---|---|
| ver | to see | the general one |
| mirar | to look at | deliberate looking |
| observar | to observe | careful, attentive |
| fijarse | to notice | *me fijé en la luz* — I noticed the light |
| asomarse | to peer / look out | leaning into a frame |

## Vocabulary

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| la luz | light |
| la sombra | shadow |
| el contraste | contrast |
| el encuadre | framing |
| la composición | composition |
| el grano | grain |
| la película | film (movie OR camera film) |
| el revelado | developing (film) |

## Voice note

PV has staggering light. Carry one of these phrases for when it floors you.
