# Hay and Había

The one-word existential. Doesn't change for singular vs plural.

## Hay — "there is / there are" (present)

- *Hay un baño cerca.* — There's a bathroom nearby.
- *Hay dos mesas libres.* — There are two free tables.
- *¿Hay wifi?* — Is there wifi?

Never *son hay* / *está hay*. Just *hay*.

## Había — "there was / there were" (imperfect)

- *Había mucha gente.* — There were a lot of people.
- *Había una luz en la ventana.* — There was a light in the window.

Imperfect carries the sense of duration / scene-setting. Pairs naturally with the imperfect tense (see [[Preterite vs Imperfect]]).

## Hubo — punctual past

- *Hubo un accidente.* — There was an accident (a specific event).

Use *hubo* when the existence is a discrete event. Use *había* for ongoing or background existence.

## Voice note

*Hay* is the workhorse for asking what's available: *¿hay café? ¿hay alguien? ¿hay otro?*
