# Prepositions

The small words that sentences hang from. Learn six and you've covered most of Day 1.

## The six

| Spanish | English | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **a** | to | destination, indirect object |
| **de** | of / from | origin, possession, material |
| **en** | in / on / at | location |
| **por** | through / by / because of | reason, route, duration |
| **para** | for / in order to | purpose, recipient, deadline |
| **con** | with | company, instrument |

## Quick contrasts

### por vs para

- *Salgo para Vallarta.* — I'm leaving FOR Vallarta. *(destination)*
- *Pasamos por el centro.* — We pass THROUGH the center. *(route)*
- *Es para usted.* — It's for you. *(recipient)*
- *Gracias por venir.* — Thanks for coming. *(reason)*

### en vs a

- *Estoy en el hotel.* — I'm AT the hotel. *(location)*
- *Voy al hotel.* — I'm going TO the hotel. *(destination)* (*a + el = al*)

### de — three jobs

- *Soy de Estados Unidos.* — I'm from the US. *(origin)*
- *El libro de María.* — María's book. *(possession)*
- *Una mesa de madera.* — A wooden table. *(material)*

## Verbs that take buscar's pattern (no preposition)

A few Spanish verbs include their preposition. Don't add one:

- *buscar* — to look FOR. *Busco mi maleta*, not *busco por mi maleta*.
- *escuchar* — to listen TO. *Escucho música*, not *escucho a música*.
- *mirar* — to look AT. *Miro la luz*, not *miro a la luz*.

## Voice note

When in doubt, listen to what Mexicans actually say. Prepositions don't translate one-for-one — they pattern.
