# TripSpanishTutor Project Space

## Working Name
Vallarta Voz / TripSpanishTutor

## Core Purpose
A literary travel-Spanish companion for a beginner learner who wants to acquire Spanish through lived travel situations and Borges's poetry, rather than through isolated drills.

The app teaches Spanish as experience:
- what I am carrying
- where I am going
- what I need
- what someone asks me
- what signs tell me
- what verbs my body is doing
- what adjectives describe my state
- what poems reveal about grammar, memory, place, and perception

## Core Metaphor
Language is not a list of words.
Language is a field of action.

Airport Spanish is not "phrases for travel."
It is a phenomenology:
- orientation
- uncertainty
- possession
- movement
- permission
- need
- politeness
- listening under pressure
- asking again without shame

Borges Spanish is not "literary decoration."
It is a high-density grammar field:
- time
- memory
- place
- mirrors
- identity
- blindness
- Buenos Aires
- nouns charged with metaphysics
- verbs that move between being, seeming, remembering, and vanishing

## Learning Style
The learner is a fast pattern-acquirer but currently a Spanish beginner.
Do not over-explain before giving usable language.
Always provide:
1. a phrase
2. literal meaning
3. natural meaning
4. grammar skeleton
5. one tiny practice move

## Primary Modes

### Airport Mode
For use while traveling.
Focus:
- directions
- signs
- airport staff questions
- customs/baggage/security
- asking for help
- listening comprehension
- polite repetition
- verbs of motion and need

### Borges Mode
For literary Spanish.
Focus:
- poem context
- grammar in the line
- vocabulary
- etymology
- image logic
- personal echo writing

### Bridge Mode
Connects travel language to literary language.
Example:
"Voy a la puerta" and Borges's "la puerta" both teach movement, threshold, and place.

## Offline Intelligence Rule
Offline mode should not depend on live LLM calls.
It should use:
- pre-cached scenelets
- phrase templates
- conjugation tables
- noun/adjective inventories
- common response patterns
- lightweight retrieval over local content packs
- optional generated fallback only when online

## Content Safety / Copyright Rule
For modern copyrighted poems, store metadata, commentary, grammar notes, learner notes, and short excerpts only where appropriate. The learner may use their own physical book while the app stores learning scaffolds around the text.

## Golden Interaction Pattern
For every phrase, poem line, or travel situation:

1. Meaning
2. Literal Spanish structure
3. Natural usage
4. Grammar skeleton
5. Practice transformation
6. Saveable card
