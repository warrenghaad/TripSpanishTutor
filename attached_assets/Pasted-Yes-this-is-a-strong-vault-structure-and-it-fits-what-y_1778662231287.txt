```md
# Puerto Vallarta Event Finding Questions

## Core questions
- How do Puerto Vallarta event, restaurant, music, art, film, and yoga communities actually broadcast what is happening?
- Which Facebook groups are most active and reliable?
- Which Instagram accounts, tags, and venue pages matter most?
- Which local websites or calendars are updated consistently?
- What information is formal calendar data versus social chatter versus flyer-level noise?
- How should the app rank and merge these sources into something useful each morning?

## Source categories
- Facebook groups and event pages
- Instagram venue accounts and local tags
- Official tourism and city event calendars
- Local live-music calendars
- Gallery and Art Walk sites
- Yoga / wellness communities
- Film screenings and open-air cinema listings
- Restaurant and bar schedule pages
- WhatsApp or concierge-style local relay channels
- Street poster / flyer / bulletin board phenomena to capture manually later

## Output goal
Build a repeatable source map the app can use for:
- live music tonight
- restaurant events
- art exhibits
- film screenings
- yoga / wellness offerings
- seasonal and civic events
- culturally meaningful happenings, not just tourist noise
```
