## 📝 Journaling Framework (tense-guided, anxiety-aware)
Use a recurring template that both records life and practices tenses. Each section shows endings or irregular forms on hover, with quick examples and audio.

### Sections and tense mapping
- What I did (Pretérito)
  - Focus: completed actions.
  - Example topics: “Fui a la galería,” “Probé tres tacos,” “Leí un capítulo.”
- What I wish I could do (Condicional or “me gustaría” + infinitive)
  - Focus: polite desire/soft intent.
  - Examples: “Me gustaría escuchar jazz en vivo,” “Querría un tour de café.”
- What I should’ve done (Condicional perfecto: “habría + participio”)
  - Focus: reflective regret without self-criticism.
  - Examples: “Habría reservado antes,” “Habría comprado boletos.”
- What I will do tomorrow (Futuro simple or “voy a + infinitivo”)
  - Focus: plan-making with small steps.
  - Examples: “Mañana visitaré el museo,” “Voy a caminar al malecón.”
- What I will have done (Futuro perfecto: “habré + participio”)
  - Focus: future accomplishments to build confidence.
  - Examples: “Para el sábado habré probado cinco platillos,” “Habré visto una película.”

### Anxiety-friendly prompts and pacing
- Micro-goals: “One small step” line in each entry (e.g., “Escribir una pregunta al guía,” “Salir 15 minutos”).
- Safety net phrasing: a designated “Plan B” sentence slot (“Si me siento abrumado, me detendré y respiraré.”).
- Gentle exposure ladder (1–5):
  1. Read about the venue in Spanish for 5 minutes.
  2. Write one sentence you might say there.
  3. Walk to the entrance and back.
  4. Enter for 10 minutes; describe one artwork or dish.
  5. Stay for a set time; engage in one interaction.

## 💬 Tense Tiles and Irregular Coaching
Make the “refrigerator poetry” mechanic earn its keep with precise guidance.

### Regular endings quick strips
- Presente
  - -ar: o, as, a, amos, áis, an
  - -er: o, es, e, emos, éis, en
  - -ir: o, es, e, imos, ís, en
- Pretérito (common regular patterns)
  - -ar: é, aste, ó, amos, asteis, aron
  - -er/-ir: í, iste, ió, imos, isteis, ieron

### Irregular spotlight (travel-friendly set)
- Tener: pretérito “tuve, tuviste, tuvo, tuvimos, tuvisteis, tuvieron”
- Hacer: pretérito “hice, hiciste, hizo…”
- Ir/Ser (suppletive): “fui, fuiste, fue…”
- Poder: pretérito “pude, pudiste, pudo…”
- Querer: pretérito “quise, quisiste, quiso…”
- Futuro perfecto/condicional perfecto: “habré/habría + participio” with participle tiles (probado, visto, escuchado, leído).

Hints:
- If a regular ending is applied to an irregular, nudge: “Casi — en pretérito ‘pud-’ no ‘pod-’: pude, pudiste…”
