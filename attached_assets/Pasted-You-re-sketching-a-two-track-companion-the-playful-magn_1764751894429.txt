## 🔍 Therapeutic Framework, Mapped to UX
This section translates ACT/DBT into repeatable app mechanics, using your “amp hum” metaphor for anxiety.

### Mindfulness: notice, name, normalize
- Prompt: “What’s the hum right now?” 
  - Short description in English, then an optional Spanish tag the app suggests (e.g., “ansiedad leve,” “tensión en el pecho”).
- Skill chips:
  - Observe: “Noticing the hum without turning the volume.”
  - Describe: “Words only; no judgments.”
  - Participate: “Small action while hum exists.”

- Coach line:
  - “Let the hum be a sound in the room, not the whole song.”

### ACT: accept and choose by values
- Values picker aligned to your interests: Books, Film, Jazz/Acoustic, Galleries, Food tours, Connection.
- Prompt sequence:
  1. Acceptance: “Acknowledge the hum.” 
     - “I can feel this and keep going.”
  2. Values: “Which value matters most right now?”
  3. Committed action: “One tiny step aligned with that value.”
     - English free-write; app offers a Spanish “nudge” sentence after you submit.

- Reframe:
  - “The amp hum is background; your chosen song is foreground.”

### DBT: distress tolerance and emotion regulation
- Distress tolerance (TIPP, Self-soothe, Pros/Cons):
  - Quick cards with one-line instructions and a 60-second timer.
- Emotion regulation:
  - Check-in (“What emotion is primary?”), plus opposite action suggestion.
- Interpersonal effectiveness (DEAR MAN):
  - Script fields for a planned interaction (e.g., asking a gallery docent a question or talking with tour members).

- Safety net:
  - “If the hum spikes, pause plan: breathe 4-7-8, step outside, text a friend.” 
  - Stored as a reusable “Plan B.”

## 📝 Bilingual Journaling Flow (English-first → Spanish blend)
Design the journal so English is fully welcome, with gentle Spanish overlays that never pressure.

### Sections
- What I noticed (mindfulness)
- What I did (past action; English; optional Spanish rendering)
- What I wish I could do (values/intent; app offers “me gustaría…” Spanish template)
- What I should’ve done (self-compassion reframing; conditional perfect suggestion)
- What I will do tomorrow (plan; “voy a…” template available)
- What I will have done (confidence projection; “habré…” template available)

### Language nudges
- After you write in English, show a small Spanish suggestion tile:
  - “Me gustaría…” for wishes
  - “Voy a…” for plans
  - “Habré…” for future perfect
- You can accept, edit, or ignore the Spanish suggestion. The app records both entries side-by-side.

### Gentle progression
- Week 1: Show Spanish only as optional translations.
- Week 2: Offer sentence starters in Spanish for 1–2 lines.
- Week 3+: Suggest mixing 50/50 English/Spanish; keep opt-out.

## 🎷 Interest-Based Prompts (books, film, jazz, galleries, food tours)
Make practice relevant to the day’s “song,” not just the hum.

- Books: “Describe one scene you loved today. If the hum rose, how did you keep reading?”
- Film: “Write a 3-line review; tag the mood during credits.”
- Jazz/Acoustic: “Notate one moment of improvisation in words; breathe with its tempo.”
- Galleries: “Observe one piece for 60 seconds; name textures and feelings.”
- Food tours: “Journal a tasting sequence; note one micro-connection with the group.”

Each prompt can surface an optional Spanish scaffold (me gustaría, probé, vi, escuché, fui).

## 🧠 The “Amp Hum” Design Pattern
Turn your metaphor into repeatable UI moments.

- Hum meter: a subtle slider with descriptors (quiet → present → loud).
- Hum journal tag: stored per entry to visualize trends.
- Befriending cue:
  - “Name the hum, soften the shoulders, return to the chosen activity.”
- Misstep reframer:
  - Instead of “I did something stupid,” prompt:
    - “I learned a thing: Next time, I’ll…” 
    - Spanish suggestion: “Aprendí algo; la próxima vez…”

## 🧩 Implementation Outline (React)
Here’s how to build it cleanly.

### Data
- prompts.json: ACT/DBT prompts keyed by interest and section.
- scaffolds.json: Spanish sentence templates per section (me gustaría, voy a, habré + participio).
- skills.json: DBT skill cards (TIPP steps, DEAR MAN fields, Self-soothe ideas).
- values.json: Interests mapped to values tags.

### Components
- JournalEntry.tsx
  - Sections with English textarea + Spanish suggestion panel.
  - Accept/ignore buttons; audio playback optional for Spanish.
- MindfulnessBar.tsx
  - Hum meter + quick breathe timer + “befriending” tip.
- ValuesPicker.tsx
- DBTSkillDeck.tsx
  - Card stack with mini timers and checkboxes.
- LanguageNudge.tsx
  - Given English summary + chosen section → propose a Spanish sentence with tense scaffold.
- ProgressView.tsx
  - Trends: hum levels, entries per value, Spanish adoption over time.

### Logic services
- nudgeGenerator.ts
  - Builds Spanish suggestions based on selected tense + interest verbs.
- tenseLibrary.ts
  - Regular endings and core irregulars for pretérito, futuro, condicional perfecto, futuro perfecto.
- safetyPlan.ts
  - Stores your personal Plan B and shows it when hum meter is “loud.”

### Storage
- Local-first (IndexedDB) with encryption option.
- Optional cloud sync for backups.
- Tag entries with date, hum level, value chosen, and whether Spanish was used.
