=============================

This is the canonical format for studying phrases suggested in chat.
A phrase is the entry point, not the destination.

MINIMUM TENSE WEB
- present: what happens / is true now
- preterite: bounded completed event
- imperfect: ongoing/background/habitual past
- present perfect: what has happened / experience relevant now
- future: what will happen
- conditional: what would happen; also softens requests
- present subjunctive: desired, uncertain, dependent, non-asserted possibilities
- imperative: direct action/request

Do not force every verb through every tense at once. Expand the tense web when the learner actually needs the contrast.

E. DICTION
----------
Explain why this word or construction fits.

DEFAULT WORD:
ALTERNATIVE 1:
DIFFERENCE:
ALTERNATIVE 2:
DIFFERENCE:
REGISTER / REGION NOTE:

Example:
Quiero... = I want...; direct and ordinary.
Quisiera... = I would like...; conditional/imperfect-subjunctive-shaped polite formula, softer.
Me gustaría... = I would like...; frames desire through gustar and often feels gentle/polite.

G. SYNTAX TRANSFORMATIONS
-------------------------
Create a small transformation ladder.

1. SAME SKELETON, NEW NOUN
2. SAME SKELETON, NEW VERB
3. CHANGE PERSON
4. CHANGE TIME
5. CHANGE POLITENESS / REGISTER
6. NEGATE IT
7. TURN STATEMENT INTO QUESTION or vice versa

Copyright rule: use metadata, commentary, learner-supplied text, public-domain text where appropriate, and only short excerpts from copyrighted works.

Poetry is evidence that grammar can carry perception. It is not decoration and it is not required on every card.

I. PRACTICE
-----------
TINY MOVE:
One transformation that takes under 30 seconds.

RECALL:
Hide the Spanish and produce it from the situation.

RECOGNITION:
Identify the verb/tense/syntax in a new sentence.

PERSONAL ECHO:
Use the structure to say something actually true or interesting to the learner.
