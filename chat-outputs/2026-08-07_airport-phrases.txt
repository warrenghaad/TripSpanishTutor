CHAT GRAMMAR SUMMARY: AIRPORT PHRASES
=====================================
DATE: 2026-08-07
MODE: Airport / travel phenomenology

CHAT PURPOSE
------------
Acquire language for orientation, movement, belongings, staff questions, listening under pressure, and polite repair.

PHRASES LEARNED
---------------
¿Dónde está la puerta B12?
Literal: Where is gate B12?
Natural: Where's gate B12?
Skeleton: ¿Dónde está + PLACE/THING?

¿Puede repetir más despacio, por favor?
Literal: Can you repeat more slowly, please?
Natural: Could you say that again more slowly?
Skeleton: ¿Puede + INFINITIVE + ADVERB/COMPLEMENT?

Estoy aprendiendo español.
Literal/Natural: I am learning Spanish.
Skeleton: estar + gerundio

Entiendo un poco, pero no todo.
Natural: I understand a little, but not everything.

Solo llevo esta mochila.
Natural: I'm only carrying this backpack.
Skeleton: llevar + direct object

¿El vuelo sale a tiempo?
Natural: Is the flight leaving on time?

¿A qué hora empieza el abordaje?
Natural: What time does boarding begin?

¿Ya están abordando?
Natural: Are they boarding already?
Skeleton: estar + gerundio

¿Dónde recojo mi equipaje?
Natural: Where do I pick up my luggage?

Mi maleta no llegó.
Natural: My suitcase didn't arrive.
Skeleton: completed event -> preterite

Estoy buscando mi maleta.
Natural: I'm looking for my suitcase.

Busco mi maleta, pero no la encuentro.
Natural: I'm looking for my suitcase, but I can't find it.

Entonces sigo los letreros y subo.
Natural: So I follow the signs and go up.
Function: confirmation by restating directions.

GRAMMAR EXTRACTED
-----------------
1. ESTAR FOR LOCATION
¿Dónde está la puerta?
Location of a thing uses estar.
Graph: ORIENTATION -> ESTAR_FOR_LOCATION

2. PODER + INFINITIVE
¿Puede repetir?
Puede is third-person singular present indicative of poder, used with usted understood.
The infinitive repetir remains unconjugated after poder.
Graph: LISTENING_REPAIR -> PODER_PLUS_INFINITIVE

3. PRESENT PROGRESSIVE
Estoy aprendiendo.
Estoy buscando.
Están abordando.
Pattern: estar conjugated + gerund.
Use when emphasizing an action in progress.
Contrast: Busco mi maleta can also naturally mean I am looking for my suitcase without progressive morphology.

4. PRETERITE FOR COMPLETED EVENT
Mi maleta no llegó.
llegó = third-person singular preterite of llegar.
The non-arrival is treated as a bounded event tied to the completed flight/arrival context.

5. DIRECT OBJECT PRONOUN
No la encuentro.
la = it, referring back to feminine singular maleta.

6. IMPERATIVE IN DIRECTIONS
Siga derecho.
Gire a la izquierda.
Suba las escaleras.
These are formal usted commands, useful for recognizing what staff say.

7. QUESTION WORDS
¿Dónde...? = where?
¿A qué hora...? = at what time?

VERBS + TENSES
---------------
estar: está / estoy / están; present indicative; location and progressive auxiliary
poder: puede; present indicative; polite ability/request frame
repetir: infinitive after poder
aprender: aprendiendo; gerund
entender: entiendo; first-person present
llevar: llevo; first-person present
salir: sale; third-person present, scheduled departure
empezar: empieza; third-person present
abordar: abordando; gerund
recoger: recojo; first-person present
llegar: llegó; third-person preterite
buscar: busco / buscando; present / gerund
 encontrar: encuentro; first-person present
seguir: sigo; first-person present
subir: subo; first-person present

VOCABULARY WEBS
----------------
PUERTA
- la puerta = door / airport gate
- puerta de embarque = boarding gate
- embarque = boarding
- abordar = to board
- salida = exit / departure
- entrada = entrance
- literary bridge: threshold

EQUIPAJE
- el equipaje = luggage, mass/collective noun
- la maleta = suitcase
- la mochila = backpack
- equipaje de mano = carry-on luggage

DIRECTIONS
- derecho = straight
- izquierda = left
- derecha = right
- después de = after
- antes de = before
- al fondo = at the end/back
- subir = go up
- bajar = go down
- seguir = follow/continue
- girar = turn

SYNTAX PATTERNS
---------------
¿Dónde está + noun?
¿Puede + infinitive + complemento?
Estoy + gerundio.
Busco + direct object.
No + direct-object-pronoun + verb.
¿A qué hora + verb + subject?
Entonces + restatement.

DICTION / REGISTER
------------------
Disculpe / Perdón soften an interruption.
¿Puede...? uses formal usted agreement and is appropriate with airport staff.
Más despacio is a compact repair request and can stand alone with por favor.
Entonces... is valuable because it confirms comprehension while creating active production.

POETRY / LITERARY BRIDGES
-------------------------
PUERTA -> threshold: the same noun can be an airport gate and a literary boundary/crossing.
SALIDA -> exit/departure: useful for literal navigation and motifs of escape/path.
Airport movement vocabulary can bridge to labyrinth/city/path imagery without changing the underlying grammar.

TINY REVIEW SET
---------------
1. Replace puerta with baño:
¿Dónde está el baño?

2. Ask someone to speak more slowly:
Más despacio, por favor.

3. Change estoy buscando to simple present:
Busco mi maleta.

4. Say the suitcase did not arrive:
Mi maleta no llegó.

5. Confirm directions using entonces:
Entonces sigo los letreros y subo.
